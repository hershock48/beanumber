# Codebase audit, October 2026

Date: 2026-10-28. Base: `origin/main` at a5a4f4b (after PR #8). Branch: `claude/audit-2026-10`.

What this is: a fine-tooth pass over the whole repo (Next.js app in `src/`, Expo app in `mobile/`, Drizzle schema, hand-written SQL in `drizzle/`, scripts, docs) for bugs, drift and bloat. The non-negotiables in `CLAUDE.md` decide what counts as a bug. Safe fixes landed as small commits on this branch; anything that changes behaviour for sponsors, money, or sponsorship-to-kid links is written up here instead, with a proposed fix, and was not changed.

Production was read, never written: three `information_schema` and `pg_indexes` queries through the Supabase connector, and the Vercel environment variable names (not values) for the `beanumber` project.

## Findings not changed, ranked

Severity: High means money, identity or a stated non-negotiable; Medium means a user-visible wrong state or a promise the product cannot keep; Low means hygiene.

### High

**H1. A sponsor who cancels loses their number.**
`src/lib/db/mutations.ts:822-855` (`cancelSponsorshipsBySubscription`, called from the webhook's `customer.subscription.deleted` handler) sets `status = 'Cancelled'`, `auth_status = 'Inactive'`, `visible_to_sponsor = false`. `core_model.md` section 0a says a sponsor who cancels recurring becomes a Holder: "status flip, no row deletion. The pick history, kid relationship, and chooser state all survive." Every reader filters on Active or Holder, so after cancelling: the magic link no longer works (`getSponsorshipEmailByCode`), `/me` is empty, the kid page treats them as a stranger, and `isNumberClaimedOutsideEmails` ignores Cancelled rows, so a different email can claim their number. Proposed: flip to `status = 'Holder'`, `monthly_amount = '0'`, keep `visible_to_sponsor = true` and the claimed number, keep the old subscription id for history. Decide with Kevin what to do with rows already Cancelled.

**H2. The gift sponsorship flow assigns a kid at purchase, from the server.**
`src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts` `assignNextShirtChild` (the `gift_sponsorship` branch) picks the lowest unassigned `children.shirt_number`, stamps `shirt_assigned_at`, `shirt_buyer_email` and `shirt_buyer_name` on the kid, and emails the recipient that number. That is the pre-matching `core_model.md` bans ("the buyer does not choose a kid at checkout, Kevin does not assign one, the system does not create a per-buyer kid pairing") and non-negotiable 1. It also only works for canonical numbers 1 to 53 (it reads `children.shirt_number`), it writes the legacy assignment columns the stockpile model abandoned, and it calls a one-time $25 a "sponsorship" (non-negotiable 2). The surface is orphaned: nothing links to `/gift` or `/gift/sponsorship` (checked nav, footer, every page). Proposed: retire `/gift`, `/gift/sponsorship`, `/gift/success`, `/api/create-gift-checkout`, the `gift_sponsorship` and `gift_shirt` webhook branches, `sendGiftCardEmail`, `sendGifterConfirmationEmail`, `assignNextShirtChild` and `mirrorGiftChildAssignment`, or rebuild it as "gift a shirt" on the stockpile model. Kevin's call; left in place.

**H3. The admin backfill creates kid-linked Active sponsorships by shirt number.**
`src/app/api/admin/backfill-subscriptions/route.ts:317-360` looks the kid up by `shirt_number` and inserts an Active row with `child_id` set, from the admin side. Non-negotiable 1 says nobody does that; the buyer claims. The route is reachable from the admin panel. Proposed: create the row childless (as the cart path does) and let the claim bind it, or delete the route if the June 2026 backfill it was written for is finished. `src/app/api/admin/stripe/sync/route.ts` has the same shape at line 323 and should get the same treatment.

**H4. Every transactional email except the magic links is sent From `Kevin@beanumber.org` by a hardcoded fallback.**
Fourteen sites read `process.env.SENDGRID_FROM_EMAIL || 'Kevin@beanumber.org'`: `src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts` (lines 498, 679, 796, 860, 926, 1049, 1250, 1347, 2752, 2839), `src/app/api/cron/drip/route.ts:643`, `src/app/api/webhooks/printful/route.ts:179`, `src/app/api/admin/messages/[id]/reply/route.ts:63`, `resend-notification/route.ts:38`, `messages/[id]/route.ts:39`, `roster/stage-candidates/route.ts:264`, `roster/upload/route.ts:265`. Production has no `SENDGRID_*` variables at all (Vercel env names checked 2026-10-28; Gmail is the only provider, `GMAIL_FROM_EMAIL` is set). `sendEmail` lets `options.from.email` override `config.fromEmail`, so these emails go out as `Kevin@beanumber.org` regardless of `GMAIL_FROM_EMAIL`. `send-link/route.ts:337-353` documents this exact fallback as the cause of the 2026-07-16 "links never arrived" outage and switched to `getEmailConfig().fromEmail`. Not changed because it alters the From header on every receipt and welcome email and I cannot verify delivery from here. Proposed: replace all fourteen with `getEmailConfig().fromEmail`, then place one test order and confirm the receipt lands.

**H5. The kid page can read another sponsor's row as the viewer's.**
`src/app/children/[number]/page.tsx` loads the newest sponsorship for the kid regardless of email (`sponsorshipPromise`, around line 1010), then only replaces it with the viewer's own row when none was found: `if (emailMatchedSponsorship && !sponsorship) sponsorship = emailMatchedSponsorship;` (around line 1100). On a kid with two sponsors the first row is often the other sponsor's, so `sponsorship_start_date`, `monthly_amount`, and the reassign-reveal trigger (`LastReassignedAt` with empty `ChildRevealedAt`) come from the wrong person. Separately, `findSponsorshipByEmailForChild` (line 381) and `donorHasActiveSponsorship` (line 602) use `ilike(sponsorEmail, email)`: ILIKE is a pattern match, so `john_doe@x.com` also matches `johnXdoe@x.com`, and it bypasses the `lower(sponsor_email)` index on the hottest page in the product. Every other email lookup in the repo uses `lower(col) = lowered`. Proposed: `if (emailMatchedSponsorship) sponsorship = emailMatchedSponsorship;` and replace both `ilike(...)` with ``sql`lower(${sponsorshipsTable.sponsorEmail}) = ${email}` ``. Identity path, so left for review.

### Medium

**M1. Two migrations were never applied in production.**
`information_schema.tables` on 2026-10-28 lists no `cohort_members` (0018, known) and no `pending_deferred_links` (0008). The deferred-link routes (`src/app/api/mobile/v1/deferred-link/stamp` and `resolve`), the kid page's `MobileAppBanner` and `mobile/lib/deepLink.ts` all depend on that table, so a first-install deep link cannot resolve. Kevin applies 0008 and 0018 in the Supabase SQL editor; both are `IF NOT EXISTS`.

**M2. Webhook-created sponsorships carry `auth_status = NULL`.**
`src/lib/db/webhook-bridge.ts` `mirrorSponsorship` and the mobile claim never set it; `src/app/api/sponsor/verify/route.ts:55` refuses anything but `'Active'`. The code login is deprecated, but `src/app/sponsor/[code]/page.tsx:92` still redirects to it, so a Stripe-created sponsor who lands there is told they have no sponsorship. Proposed: default `auth_status` to `'Active'` in `createSponsorship`, or remove the verify route with the deprecated surface.

**M3. The mobile claim re-implements the web claim without the second-claim guard.**
`src/app/api/mobile/v1/claim/route.ts:150-215` duplicates `bindOrCreateHolderClaim` from `src/lib/claim-number.ts` with two differences (stamps `childRevealedAt`, picks the linked purchase email first) and none of the 2026-10-28 guard. The PR #8 hand-over chose that deliberately (the app has an explicit "Keep #N" tap), but it means a typo in the app still mints a Holder row with no purchase behind it. Proposed: give `bindOrCreateHolderClaim` a `revealedNow` option and call it from the mobile route; decide with Kevin whether the tap counts as the confirmation.

**M4. The single-shirt + monthly path creates a subscription with no sponsorship row.**
The `shirt_plus_monthly` branch of the webhook records the donation and the Stripe subscription and then says "Kevin creates the Sponsorship row + issues the sponsor code manually after shipping." The cart path, by contrast, creates a childless Active row the claim later binds. The two routes that emit `shirt_plus_monthly` (`/api/create-shirt-checkout`, `/api/create-market-checkout`) have no callers in the site or the app (every button posts to the cart variants), so today the branch only serves sessions minted before the cart switch. Proposed: make the branch mirror the cart path (`createSponsorshipFromCartCheckout`) so a replayed old session never leaves a paying buyer without a row, and delete the two routes (see dead code).

**M5. Drip enrolment depends on Stripe event order for cart + monthly.**
`customer.subscription.created` usually arrives before `checkout.session.completed`. Cart subscriptions carry no `order_type` in `subscription_data.metadata`, so the subscription handler enrols the donor in `monthly_donor`; the checkout handler then overwrites that with `shirt_sponsor`. If the events arrive the other way round the buyer stays on `monthly_donor` and never gets "have you met your kid yet". Proposed: put `order_type: 'cart'` on the cart subscription metadata and skip drip changes for it in the subscription handler.

**M6. The sponsor welcome promises contradict each other.**
`src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts` welcome email: "monthly photos" in one paragraph, "photos of {child} every few months" two paragraphs later. "Monthly photos" appears in 13 places across emails, PenpalBox, `/me` and the app; `sponsor/welcome` says "over the coming months". Which one is operationally true is Kevin's call; the copy should then say it everywhere.

**M7. Simon and "Hope Bridge" on the Christmas surfaces.**
`src/app/christmas/page.tsx:36`, `christmas/success/page.tsx:39, 57`, `children/[number]/ChristmasGiftCard.tsx:101` and the Christmas receipt email name Simon as the delivery mechanic, which `known_gotchas.md` says never to do on sponsor-facing surfaces, and they call the campus "Hope Bridge", which a comment on the kid page (line 2239) calls off-brand. These shipped in PRs #4 and #6 on 2026-10-22 as Kevin's own copy, so they were left alone. Kevin decides which rule wins; if the gotcha does, replace with "the team at the campus" and "the campus".

**M8. "Empower" on the YDO partner pages.**
Seven user-facing lines on `/ydo`, `/ydo/about` and `/ydo/programs` (metadata and body) use "empower" or "empowering", which `voice.md` bans standing alone. They quote YDO's own mission statement, so they were left for Kevin: either YDO's wording wins on YDO's pages, or they get the rewrites in the copy audit (scratch report, section 5).

**M9. The deprecated sponsor surfaces still promise things the product does not do.**
`src/app/sponsor/welcome/page.tsx:33-35` says an email with "your sponsor code, your child's profile, and a link to the sponsor portal" arrives within a business day; `:41-42` and `:114-115` promise "a handwritten letter once a year" (the exact phrase `voice.md` says to avoid); `:29` headline "You just changed a life." (savior framing). See the dead code section for what happened to these files.

**M10. Nine `AIRTABLE_*` variables are still set in Vercel.**
No code reads them since 2026-09-14 and `CLAUDE.md` says not to add them back; they are harmless but stale. Kevin deletes them in the Vercel project settings.
