Be A Number — bag labels

Type the sizes you want, comma-separated. One label per size. Repeat a size to print multiple.

Examples:   M  ·  S, M, M, L, L, XL  ·  M, M, M, M, M, M

Print settings: paper size 4 × 2 in (or whatever your label stock is — match the @page CSS at the top of this file), margins None, background graphics On.
