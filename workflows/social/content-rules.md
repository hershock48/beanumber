# Social Media Content Rules

## Objective

Define the posting rules and guidelines for Be A Number's social media presence.

## Posting Frequency

| Rule | Default Value | Configurable |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| Max posts per day | 2 | Yes |
| Min hours between posts | 4 | Yes |
| Preferred post times | 9:00 AM, 6:00 PM | Yes |
| Timezone | America/New_York | Yes |

## Content Guidelines

### Caption Requirements
- **Required**: Yes, all posts must have a caption
- **Max length**: 2,200 characters (Instagram limit)
- **Recommended length**: 150-300 characters
- **Call to action**: Include when appropriate
- **Emojis**: Use sparingly, max 3-5 per post

### Hashtag Requirements
- **Required**: Yes for Instagram
- **Max hashtags**: 30 (Instagram limit)
- **Recommended**: 5-15 relevant hashtags
- **Brand hashtags**: Always include #BeANumber

## Approval Workflow

### Require Approval
- **Production default**: Yes
- **Approver**: Admin (Kevin@beanumber.org)

### Approval Process
1. Content is scheduled with status "Pending"
2. Admin reviews via dashboard or Airtable
3. Admin approves (status stays "Pending" for cron to pick up)
4. Or admin cancels with reason

### Auto-Approval (Future)
Certain content types may be auto-approved:
- Re-posts of previously approved content
- Updates using approved templates
- Posts from verified admin accounts

## Rate Limits

### Meta API (2026)
- 200 API calls per hour per account
- ~25 posts per day (soft limit)
- 30 hashtags per post (hard limit)

### Best Practices
- Don't post more than 2x per day
- Space posts at least 4 hours apart
- Monitor engagement to optimize timing

## Content Calendar Integration

### Regular Posts
- **Monthly impact update**: 1st of each month
- **Weekly highlight**: Every Friday
- **Event coverage**: As they happen

### Seasonal Content
- **Year-end recap**: December
- **Giving Tuesday**: November
- **School year milestones**: September, January, June

## Quality Standards

### Images
- High resolution (min 1080px width)
- Good lighting
- Faces visible and smiling
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Consent obtained for all subjects

### Videos
- H.264 MP4 format
- 30fps recommended
- Clear audio (if any)
- Subtitles/captions for accessibility
- 9:16 aspect ratio for Reels/Stories

### Captions
- Clear and concise
- Dignity-based (not pity-based)
- Action-oriented
- Include website/link when relevant
- No typos or grammatical errors

## Prohibited Content

- Content without proper consent
- Graphic or distressing imagery
- Political statements unrelated to mission
- Personal information of children
- Unverified statistics or claims
- Negative portrayal of communities served
