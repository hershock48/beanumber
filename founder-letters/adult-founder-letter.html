Howdy!

Somehow I started this thing about 16 years ago in my college dorm and never stopped.

It took me all over the world and eventually I found myself in the post-war zone of Northern Uganda, by myself, living for free with immigrants. It was a profound experience. I started a bakery to help women escape prostitution, distributed micro-loans, and helped people start small businesses with the help of local leaders.

Eventually, my friend and I were able to purchase 6 acres of land — no running water and no electricity. Over time we have built up a growing campus, partnering with tribal leaders and elders. Our campus includes an elementary school with the capacity for 360 students, a medical facility (malaria is a big problem where we are), and a guest house (come visit!).

The shirt you’re holding is a product of many sleepless nights and failed experiments. Thank you for believing in doing good, human connection, and making the world a smaller, more peaceful place.

Reach out anytime and I am so happy you’re a Number.

Welcome to the family.

Ever,

Kevin Hershock

Founder, Be A Number
